09

Study Significance

This study provides empirical evidence to help schools and policymakers identify Ethical & Responsible AI Governance as a priority area for strengthening headteachers’ digital leadership competencies, particularly through professional development focused on the ethical and responsible use of AI.

10 · Final takeaway

“Effective AI-oriented digital leadership is not only about encouraging AI adoption and innovation; it also requires the capacity to govern AI ethically, responsibly, and accountably.”
